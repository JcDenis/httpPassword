Authorized users
Add at least one login and password before enabling the protection. To change a password, type the new one on the user line, then click "Change password". Never click with an empty field.
Settings
Check the box to ask visitors for a login and password on every public page. Choose "Crypt MD5", "Crypt SHA256" or "Crypt SHA512": visitors cannot log in with "No encryption" or "Crypt Blowfish". After a change of algorithm, save each password again. The message is shown in the browser login window.
Logins history
Shows the login and the date of the last connection of an authorized user. "Clear logs" empties the list.
